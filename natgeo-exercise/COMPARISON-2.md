## The finding that matters: features travel

Neither of my round-two pieces leaves its location. Both real ones are built as journeys.

The dinosaur feature runs Yucatán mangroves → Los Angeles → Kemmerer, Wyoming → back to the
Yucatán rim. The archives feature is literally three countries, with the place names used as
the subheads: **Kurdistan. Somaliland. Kosovo.**

This is structural and I missed it in both rounds. A Geographic feature is frequently a
**circuit**: two or three widely separated places doing versions of the same thing, cut
against each other, with the reporter physically moving between them. That is where the
travel, the access and the comparison all come from, and it is why the writer's "I" is so
prominent: somebody has to be the thing that moves.

My pieces put the comparison inside one location and imported the second site as testimony
(Réunion via a phone call, Timbuktu via one interview). The real form goes there.

## Rule 5 is validated hard

Round one's biggest correction was putting the reporter back in. The archives piece confirms
it beyond the raw percentage. It documents access explicitly: meetings "granted
conditionally", elders reluctant to open collections, negotiations "lasting hours with
minimal results", a Sheikh refusing to disclose the size of his own collection, the whole
thing described by the writer as "a familiar dance".

That is precisely what rule 5 says the first person is for, and it is the single strongest
confirmation in either round. My warzone piece does this with the checkpoint and the
conditions of access, and it was the right instinct.

But **my band is too low.** Both real features are described as heavy or prominent in first
person. Mine ran 9% and 12-16%. The band should be 10-25%.

## Endings: they close on a quote

Both round-two articles, and one from round one, end on a source speaking.

> "It's almost like they're knitted together," she said. "No one ever tells the past without
> telling the present."

> "They need to say a little prayer and get the hell out of there," Angelillo said.

I have never once done this. All five of my features close on narration. Ending on a quote
hands the last word to the person the story is about rather than to the writer, and it is
evidently a house move.

## Where I still over-deliver

**Legal and institutional machinery.** The archives piece does not discuss conventions,
frameworks or repatriation law at all, and it is a story about saving archives. My warzone
piece is built on the 1954 Hague Convention. Same finding as round one's mechanism problem,
in a new costume: I reach for the system, they reach for the person.

I am not sure they are right. A story about whether to mark a building is a story about a
treaty. But it is a deviation and it should be declared, like the unresolved-disagreement one.

**Prices.** The Liaoning coverage gives no fossil prices, only "peasant farmers make only a
few hundred dollars a year". My price chain is more detailed than the form uses. Rule 13
defends it and I would keep it.

## Where the model is less consistent than I assumed

**Unresolved disagreement.** Round one found none in Mosquitia and none in Cape Cod, and I
concluded it was my deviation. Round two splits: the dinosaur feature carries a live,
unsettled dispute about bird origins; the archives feature has none. So it is available in
the form, used sometimes. My insistence is still a deviation, but a smaller one than I
thought.

## Corrections for v3

1. **Words: 4,000-5,500.** My floor was too low and I have now written five features at or
   near the bottom of whatever range I set.
2. **First person: 10-25%,** and it must carry access explicitly, including refusals.
3. **Go somewhere else.** Build the piece as a circuit of two or three locations. Do not
   import the second site by phone.
4. **Consider closing on a quote.** Not mandatory, but it is a move I have never used and
   they use constantly.
5. **Ages are allowed** at about a quarter of the cast. Round one's correction was too hard.
6. **Declare the institutional-machinery deviation** the way the disagreement one is declared.
