# Evidence spine

- Eastern Mediterranean seasonal winds, currents, coastal sailing, and open-water crossings.
- The Uluburun wreck’s copper and tin ingots, Canaanite jars, resin, glass, ivory, foodstuffs, personal effects, shipboard wares, and surviving hull.
- Water, fuel, stowage, spoilage, and cargo weight as practical limits on a voyage.
- Chickpeas, olives, grain, fruit, and other botanical remains recovered from Late Bronze Age maritime contexts.

## Working limits

The voyage, captain, Yabninu, dialogue, and chickpea meal are invented from the physical constraints. No shipboard menu survives. Uluburun yielded galley wares, but the article does not claim that its small guarded fire is a preserved fixture from that wreck.
