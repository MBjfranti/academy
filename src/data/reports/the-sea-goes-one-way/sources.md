# Evidence spine

- Eastern Mediterranean seasonal winds, currents, coastal sailing, and open-water crossings.
- The Uluburun wreck’s copper, tin, jars, luxury cargo, and botanical remains.
- Portable braziers or fireboxes as the safe limit for cooking aboard a timber vessel.
- Water, fuel, spoilage, and weight as route constraints.

## Declared reconstruction

The voyage, captain, cook, dialogue, and chickpea meal are invented from the physical constraints. No shipboard menu survives.
