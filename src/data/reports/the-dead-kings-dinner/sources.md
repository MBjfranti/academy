# Evidence spine

- Egyptian daily temple ritual texts and offering lists.
- The reversion and redistribution of offerings to temple staff.
- Temple estates, workshops, and personnel that supplied cult meals.
- Private tomb chapels, funerary endowments, and family offerings to the dead.
- Fowl, bread, beer, meat, wine, honey, and coriander in Egyptian food evidence.

## Declared reconstruction

Henut’s kitchen procedure, the minor noble’s household, and her visit to her husband’s chapel are narrative reconstructions. No surviving source gives this duck recipe as a continuous method.
