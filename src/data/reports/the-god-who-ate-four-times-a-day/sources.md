# Evidence spine

- Babylonian temple ritual and administrative texts for the regular meals of major gods.
- Offering distribution among priests and temple personnel.
- YBC 8958, the damaged Old Babylonian tablet containing elaborate bird preparations.
- The political hierarchy expressed by divine seating and portions.

## Chronology note

Balāṭu reads the Old Babylonian bird tablet as an antiquarian object. It predates his first-millennium temple setting by many centuries.

## Declared reconstruction

The dough-sealed pigeon pot is a working reading of broken instructions. Balāṭu’s trials and kitchen incidents are invented.
