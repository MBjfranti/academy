# Evidence spine

- Mycenaean plain stemmed cups and large drinking-vessel deposits at palace sites.
- Pylos Linear B allocations of livestock, grain, wine, oil, honey, and spices.
- The Pylos megaron, central hearth, libation channel, and painted interior.
- Ceramic firedogs and bronze skewers as evidence for hearth cooking.

## Declared reconstruction

Alektryon, his carts, the visiting captain, the feast, and every exchange with Anniwiya are invented. The article uses the cups and allocations to reconstruct the scale and social order of palace feasting. Anniwiya’s movement between royal and working spaces belongs to her declared fictional biography.
